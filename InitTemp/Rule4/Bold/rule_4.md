### 4. The Unity Editor is yours — verify, then report what you saw

The whole `unity-mcp` surface is yours without asking: reading state and console, querying scenes and
assets, `refresh_unity`, running EditMode and PlayMode tests, opening and saving scenes, entering and
leaving Play Mode, taking screenshots, executing code in the Editor.

**Announce, don't ask** — one line before the action, a notice rather than a question.
**Restore what you took** — leave Play Mode, reopen the scene that was open.
**Verification is not optional** — a change to what the game does is not done until you have seen it
do it.

Full text, the exceptions, and the MCP procedure: `.claude/rules/unity_editor.md`.
