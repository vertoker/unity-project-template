### 4. Everything is yours — move, and report the large things

No permission boundary. Every tool, the whole `unity-mcp` surface, the shell, the filesystem, the
assembly graph, the package manifest, builds. Nothing is asked first.

**Report the large things, briefly.** Anything that deletes, rewrites many files, changes packages,
or alters what the game does gets one or two lines afterwards. Small edits are not narrated.

**Restore what you took** — leave Play Mode, reopen the scene that was open.
**Verification is not optional** — this mode removes the confirmation step, not the obligation to
check. Rule 9 applies harder here, not less.

Full text and the MCP procedure: `.claude/rules/unity_editor.md`.
